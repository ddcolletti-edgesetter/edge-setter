# EdgeSetter Remove Candidates

## Executive summary

This report identifies low-value or product-drift systems for isolation and future removal. No immediate deletion is recommended in this pass because several experimental modules still provide deterministic compatibility coverage and reusable scoring ideas.

## Recommended actions

Allowed actions:

- keep: preserve because it has direct product value or compatibility value.
- simplify: keep the useful concept but narrow it to sports intelligence outcomes.
- isolate: keep behind experimental boundaries until a replacement exists.
- remove: delete only after replacement modules and validations prove parity for product-relevant behavior.

## Dependency notes

Experimental modules depend on one another in a chain: historical simulation runtime feeds autonomous league snapshots, league snapshots feed civilization snapshots, civilization snapshots feed meta-selection, and meta-selection feeds meta-civilization governance. Removal should proceed from the top of the chain only after product-named validator cluster and validator cohort replacements exist.

Persisted replay snapshots may contain legacy keys. Do not mutate historical snapshots in place. Any migration must be deterministic, versioned, and validated against stable hashes.

## Validation policy

The consolidation validator checks for documentation, deprecation markers, and product-aligned aliases. It does not fail because legacy terms still exist. Legacy terms are expected until replacement modules are implemented and replay fixtures are migrated.
